General information only

The licence and endorsement information on this site is a plain-English summary to help you plan. The rules are set by NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi (NZTA) and can change, so check the details that matter to you with NZTA or a driver licensing agent. Nothing on this site is legal advice.

Courses, prices and bookings

Course prices, dates, locations and booking terms are confirmed directly with Brar Transport Training when you book, by phone or email. Any fees charged by NZTA for adding an endorsement to your licence are separate from the course fee and are paid to NZTA.

A course certificate is not an endorsement. You still need to apply to have the endorsement added to your driver licence before you drive a special-type vehicle on a road.
