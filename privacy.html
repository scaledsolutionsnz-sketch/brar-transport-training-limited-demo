Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

This website has no forms and no sign-ups, and it does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. The map near the bottom of the home page is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies. If you call or email us, we use your details to answer you and to arrange your training. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

When you contact us, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, phone number, email address and which endorsements you are interested in. When you book a course, we may also need details such as your driver licence information to arrange your training and your course paperwork.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and book your course.
  • To prepare your course paperwork.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law.

We only share your information when it is needed to arrange your training or when the law requires it.

Third-party services on this site

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows a Google Map. When your browser loads these, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, under Google’s own privacy policy. The email button opens Gmail in a new tab; anything you send that way is handled by your email provider and then by us.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and only keep it for as long as we need it.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Call 021 187 1831 or email brartransporttraining@outlook.com. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
