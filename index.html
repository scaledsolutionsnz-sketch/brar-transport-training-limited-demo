Thames based

Local training for a working industry

Brar Transport Training Limited runs licence courses and driver training out of Thames, for people across the Hauraki, Coromandel and wider Waikato districts.

Training is one on one. You book a time that suits, learn at your own pace, and get straight answers about what each licence takes, what it costs and how long it needs.

  • One on one lessons, no crowded classrooms
  • A local instructor who knows the roads you will be tested on
  • Clear pricing and course times before you commit
